Football

About the Sport

Football, also known as soccer in some countries, is a sport played by two teams of eleven players with a spherical ball. It is the most popular sport in the world, with billions of fans and players globally. I love football because of its speed, skill, and teamwork, which make every game exciting to watch and play.

Basic Rules

  • The game is played on a rectangular field with two goalposts at each end.
  • The objective is to score by getting the ball into the opposing team's goal.
  • Each team has 11 players, including a goalkeeper.
  • The match is played in two 45-minute halves, with a 15-minute break in between.
  • Only the goalkeeper is allowed to use their hands, and only within the penalty area.
